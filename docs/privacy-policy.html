Privacy Policy

App: Private Statistics  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-21

🇩🇪 Deutsche Version

Private Statistics is a personal event-tracking app that helps you log events, spot patterns, and understand your habits over time. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data stored on your device

All event records, categories, and settings you create in the app are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device. This data is never transmitted to any server and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

You can export or delete your data at any time using the Backup & Restore feature inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

2. Health Connect

If you grant permission, the app reads the following health data types from Android Health Connect: Steps, Sleep sessions, and Workout sessions.

  • Health data is read read-only and used solely to enrich your local statistics.
  • Health data is never transmitted outside the app — not to the developer, not to any analytics service, and not to any third party.
  • Health data is not persisted in the app's own database; it is fetched on demand and displayed only within the app.

You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time via Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Private Statistics.

3. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymous crash reports when the app crashes unexpectedly.

  • Crash reports contain technical diagnostics only: stack traces, device model, Android version, and app version.
  • Crash reports do not contain any personal data, event records, health data, or any other user content.
  • No analytics events, advertising identifiers, or behavioural data are collected.

Crashlytics data is processed by Google LLC under their Firebase privacy terms.

4. No advertising or third-party data sharing

The app contains no advertising and does not share any data with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics platforms. No user profiling takes place.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
